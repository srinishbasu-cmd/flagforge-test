Bridging the Gap in Cybersecurity Education

Professional cybersecurity certifications are expensive and often out of reach for students and early-career learners. At the same time, most free learning platforms teach concepts individually but rarely test whether a learner can combine multiple tools the way a real engagement demands.

Flag Forge is a free, open, and contribution-driven space dedicated to cybersecurity CTFs and practical learning. We build progressively harder, hands-on challenges that mirror what professional certifications actually test—without being the certification itself.

Accessibility, Not Replacement

This is not a substitute for certification. It is a bridge for people who cannot yet afford one. Through our challenges, learners can practice exact skill categories, work with industry-standard tools, and attempt realistic, scenario-based problems.

Every challenge is original content, built by our contributors, reviewed, and freely available. We are explicit about this boundary: completing our challenges proves practical skill, not certification.
